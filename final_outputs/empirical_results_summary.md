# Empirical Results Summary

Claim boundary: these results are based on a centroid travel-time proxy. They
support a reproducible potential-accessibility screening analysis. OSRM-routed
travel times should replace the proxy before making routed road-network claims.

## Coverage

The analytic panel contains 1,123
municipalities and 5,615
municipality-year rows. In 2024, the spatial gap is non-missing for
1,122
municipalities.

## Main 2024 pattern

The highest 2024 spatial gap in the current proxy is Bojayá,
Chocó (`27099`), with `brecha_spatial_z =
4.73`. The department with the highest mean municipal
gap is Vichada (`mean_gap_z = 4.02`),
while the lowest mean gap is in Bogotá, D.C. (`mean_gap_z =
-2.17`).

## Spatial clustering

The queen-contiguity Moran's I for the 2024 gap is 0.642
using 3,277 undirected neighbour pairs
(`p_greater = 0.002`, 499 permutations).
This supports reporting spatial clustering descriptively, not causally.
